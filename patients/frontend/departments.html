Patient Navigation

Navigating your specialist visit at Hopewell

Each clinical department is organized into integrated care units where physicians, nurse specialists, and technicians work side-by-side to minimize unnecessary transfers between suites.

1

Schedule Online or Request a Specialist

Browse by department, select your preferred physician or appointment time slot, and receive a digital reference token.

2

Track Wait Time Before Leaving Home

On your visit date, enter your token into our Live Queue tracker to monitor room progress and check-in timing.

3

Check In Directly at Department Front Desk

Present your confirmation code to our outpatient reception desk in the designated clinical wing for rapid triage.
