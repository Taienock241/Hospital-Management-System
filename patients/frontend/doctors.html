Guidance & Support

Consultation Guidelines & FAQs

Answers to common questions regarding specialist visits, booking handoffs, and queue check-in.

Do I need a physician referral to book an appointment?

Most outpatient clinics (including Internal Medicine and Pediatrics) do not require a referral. Specific sub-specialties like Neurosurgery or Oncology may request prior laboratory or primary physician records.

How does the live queue integration work with my booking?

After a successful booking, use the generated reference while signed in to view queue information on the appointment day. Wait estimates are not shown unless supported by current clinic data.

What should I bring to my initial consultation?

Please bring a valid photo identification card, a list of current prescription medications, previous diagnostic scans or lab results, and your appointment confirmation code.

Can I request a virtual telehealth consultation instead?

Yes. Several specialists support video-based triage and follow-up consultations. Select the "Virtual Consult" option on any qualifying doctor card during the booking step.
